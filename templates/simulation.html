What each library does here

  • NumPy: generates random gaps between student arrivals and computer-use durations using an exponential distribution.
  • SimPy: advances a simulated clock and treats each available computer as a shared resource students may wait to use.
  • pandas: groups the repeated simulation results and summarizes the average and 95th-percentile measures. It also groups saved observations for the dashboard’s historical probability.

This is a what-if model, not a live forecast. Arrival rates and session durations are assumptions entered above; use real campus observations to calibrate them. It does not change the database or reserve computers.
